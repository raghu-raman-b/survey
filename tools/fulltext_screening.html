Full-text screening. Each of you screens your own half, on your own device.
  1. Load fulltext/fulltext_corpus.json and criteria.json.
  2. Pick yourself. Only your papers are shown, and only the ones you have.
  3. For each paper: find it in Zotero → Got it → answer one check at a time. The first check it fails is the exclusion reason; pass them all and it's included.
  4. Export fulltext_<name>.json into fulltext/. When both are there, run scripts/12_fulltext_combine.py to fill in the PRISMA diagram.

1Corpus

The full-text corpus file: every paper kept at title/abstract, who retrieved it, and what is already settled from the retrieval notes.

Drop fulltext_corpus.json hereor click to choose

2Criteria

The same criteria.json as before. Its skim guide sets the order of the checks; the RQs show up as hints under the checks they belong to.

Drop criteria.json hereor click to choose

3Who is screening?

Pick your name. The list then holds only your papers.

Load the corpus first.

4Save and export

Decisions are kept in this browser only. Export when you stop for the day too, so a cleared browser can't lose your work. To continue on another browser, import your last export.
