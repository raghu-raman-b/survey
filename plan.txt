Generative AI in XR


Scope: Learned generative models (LLMs, GANs, diffusion, VLMs, etc.) that are central to the XR (VR, AR, MR) experience. Post-2021 (should also cover apt pre-2021 works like procedural generation). Systematic Review.


Contributions: (1) Taxonomy of generation techniques (2) Interaction Taxonomy (3) Cost-benefit evaluation of Generative XR (4) Analysis of how generative AI affects user experience (5) Domain analysis of GenAI adoption.


1. Generation: Technologies used to generate XR environments and assets 
        
* Taxonomy of generation technologies


        What is generated and how is it generated?
        
* What is generated? Output type (Scenes, assets, sound, texture, etc.)
* How is it generated? Model family (LLMs, VLMs, GANs, diffusion, etc.) and infrastructure (cloud, edge, on-device, model scale)
* When it is generated? (offline or runtime)

3. Evaluation: Cost-benefit analysis of Generative XR systems and experience optimization


        3a. Cost-benefit analysis


* Define what is cost (computational expense, latency, resources) and define benefit (quality, performance) 
* Catalogue metrics in literature
   * Catalogue of content quality metrics used to evaluate generated outputs.
   * Catalogue of system evaluation metrics used to evaluate performance and computational resources of generative XR systems
* Apply cost-benefit analysis to: compare technologies within generation taxonomy, compare traditional XR systems against generative XR systems, compare interaction approaches.


        3b. Experience Optimization
* Catalogue of UX metrics used in the Generative XR literature: subjective (presence, engagement), cognitive (cybersickness, cognitive load), etc. 
* Does generative AI improve, or degrade experience compared to non-generative systems?
* Which attributes of generative systems aid in positive experience? (personalization, for instance)
* Which new risks are introduced? (like uncanny valley effects, determinism in generation)
* Relationship between generation quality / interaction with user experience


4. Synthesis: 
* Gap analysis and future directions. (limitations)
* Relationship to existing surveys.

Research Questions


RQ1: What generative AI and traditional content-creation techniques are applied in XR, and how are they distributed across output types, model architectures, computational infrastructure, generation timing, and application domains?


RQ2: What interaction techniques in XR involve generative AI, what role does GenAI play in each, and how do these interaction paradigms compare to those in non-generative XR systems?


RQ3: What computational costs (latency, resource use) and benefits (content quality, performance) are reported for generative XR systems, and how do these compare to prior non-generative approaches such as procedural generation?


RQ4: How does generative AI affect user experience in XR (presence, engagement, cognitive load, cybersickness) compared to non-generative systems, and what new risks does it introduce (uncanny valley, inconsistency across sessions)?
